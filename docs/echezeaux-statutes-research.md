# Échezeaux: company deeds, dated tenancies and next source requests

Reviewed 29 September 2026. Facts are curated in [the research data](research/echezeaux-farming-curation.json); the original PDFs are linked below, not redistributed.

Six uploaded PDFs now establish contributions or transfers and dated lease relationships for sixteen exact parcels. All sixteen cadastral areas match the pinned parcel data. Coudray and the HOR transfer add four previously unnamed tenant leads; HOR's cash-only formation adds chronology, not a parcel lease. These records strengthen identities beyond shared offices and family names. They do not alone verify farming in the 2026 season.

Page references below are one-based PDF pages. ORVEAUX's printed page numbers are one lower than its PDF page numbers.

The deed is dated **2 July 2024** (PDF p. 1); **10 July 2024** is the date in the Pappers copy label. Registry SIREN: 930915806. Matching DGFiP holder: U33046436.

**Sale chronology, p. 10:** the brothers bought these assets together, half each, on **14 March 2024**, then contributed them to the GFA. The document describes acquisition with other assets but does not identify those other assets here. This establishes why the sale lead must remain limited: the later GFA holder cannot be labeled the known buyer of the March sale. D0146 is absent from the contribution tables; its occurrence in the same DVF transaction remains a separate lead, not proof of a contribution or lease to this GFA.

**Lease evidence, pp. 12-13:** the deed recites the earlier long-term metayage lease to Emmanuel Rouget and Mireille Jantet, executed 10 February 1987, amended 16 April 1998, effective 11 November 1986. It describes nine-year renewals and a scheduled 10 November 2031 end. It also records a **13 June 2017** landlord authorization to make the property available to **DOMAINE EMMANUEL ROUGET, EARL, SIREN 809967854**.

The sentence continuing onto p. 13 expressly refers to a **concurrently signed new long-term lease to Nicolas and Guillaume Rouget**. Therefore do not present the old Emmanuel/Mireille lease as unchanged through 2031. Page 33 mandates the new lease over the contributed parcels, from signature to **10 November 2048**, with permission to make them available to the same EARL, written there as DOMAINE ROUGET EMMANUEL, SIREN 809967854. The statutes supply strong dated evidence and recite the concurrent lease; the separate executed lease has not been reviewed.

**Data treatment:** distinguish individual March buyers, July land contribution, individual tenants, and the EARL receiving the permitted mise a disposition. The exact operating entity identified by the filing is **809967854**, not the different Rouget company **834133936** used in the earlier address comparison. Retain the older registry source for its limited purpose, but do not use it as the lease counterparty.

## ORVEAUX

The deed is dated **9 February 2024** (PDF p. 2); the copy label says **12 February 2024**. Page 4 names the company **Groupement Foncier Viticole de "ORVEAUX"**, reconciling the longer DGFiP name. Registry SIREN: 984543470. Matching holder: U32852627.

**Contribution and area, PDF p. 5 / printed p. 4:** Robert and Francoise Jousset-Drouhin contribute the following En Orveaux parcels. The deed explicitly distinguishes cadastral area from the **actual, plantable and planted area of 4,147 m2 (0.4147 ha)**.

The 0.4147 ha figure provides a direct explanation consistent with the importer's rounded 0.41 ha. The older 52.63-are figure and the current smaller vineyard figure need not represent a land sale or erroneous parcel match. Preserve all three area measures with their meanings; the filing does not explain the difference between the lease's enumerated 4,550 m2 and the 4,147 m2 plantable/planted area.

**Tenant, PDF p. 6 / printed p. 5:** a long-term rural lease executed **20 December 2003**, published 11 January 2005, names **Laurent JOUSSET-DROUHIN personally**. The document refers to the tenant in place and no ongoing landlord dispute. It does not name Domaine Joseph Drouhin's operating company as the direct tenant.

The annexed SAFER notification, PDF pp. 33-34, identifies the same tenant. It records a lease entry date of **1 November 2003**, an **18-year** duration, and renewal by tacit reconduction. Its broad occupancy field says a total lease, while the deed's detailed lease recital enumerates partial areas for three parcels. Keep that difference visible; the notification is not a basis to overwrite the detailed partial-parcel scope.

**Data treatment:** the exact four-parcel identity is now supported by the deed, independently of the historical Hachette area reconstruction. Add the contribution and dated lease evidence separately from the older guide entry. Keep Laurent's personal tenancy distinct from an inference about the operating domaine, and retain the historical guide's own date rather than assigning it the incorporation date.

## HOR VIGNOBLES and SCI LES CLIMATS

The **8 October 2021** filing bundle contains the previously missing transfer document: a **30 June 2021** notarial capital-reduction deed, with an attribution in kind to the withdrawing shareholder **SCI LES CLIMATS, SIREN 798977476**. HOR VIGNOBLES is SIREN 821877008. The company resolution is dated **31 May 2021**; PDF p. 8 expressly puts the property transfer and possession on the date of the deed, **30 June 2021**.

**Transferred land, PDF p. 7 / printed p. 4:** **D0815, En Orveaux, 856 m2 (0.0856 ha)**, expressly AOC Echezeaux. The deed says D0815 comes from division of former **D0315, 4,273 m2**. HOR acquired that parent on **25 October 2016**, published **10 November 2016**, Dijon 4, volume **2016P**, number **3871** (PDF pp. 7-8). Those publication details are a precise pointer for requesting the original acquisition deed and parcel schedule.

**Tenant, PDF p. 9 / printed p. 6:** under *Etat des locations*, the transferred property is leased to **DOMAINE DU CHATEAU DE MARSANNAY, SIREN 305242687**, under a **viticultural metayage lease executed 25 October 2016**. It recites a retroactive start of **1 September 2016**, an end of **31 October 2040**, and describes the duration as **24 years**. Preserve these printed dates and duration as recited; do not silently calculate a different end date. The full executed 2016 lease is not attached here.

**Scope:** this tenancy clause refers to the property transferred in this deed, D0815. The annexed cadastral plan on **PDF p. 30** also labels neighboring D0813 and D0814, but a plan is not a tenancy schedule. Neither their adjacency nor the parent-parcel recital extends the Marsannay tenant assignment to those parcels. HOR's indexed accounts describe 0.3417 ha, arithmetically consistent with 4,273 minus 856 m2, but that is supporting ownership-area context, not evidence of a tenant for D0813/D0814.

**Chronology correction:** the annual DGFiP files show the recorded holder switching from HOR to SCI Les Climats between the **2022 and 2023 snapshots**. The deed now independently dates the transfer to **30 June 2021**. Keep both facts, with their different meanings. The earlier research title dating the transaction to 2022 was too strong and is corrected; the pinned annual observations themselves are unchanged. The reason for the later appearance in the annual records is not established.

**Data treatment:** D0815 gains a dated Domaine du Chateau de Marsannay tenant lead. This supersedes the earlier unresolved-company status, not the requirement to verify actual current farming. D0813/D0814 stay without named tenant candidates.

### HOR formation, 1 August 2016

The third uploaded file, `821877008-63def6cbc361ed1a931117a6.pdf`, is the **19-page formation filing**, registered **5 August 2016**. The statutes are signed **1 August 2016** (PDF p. 16 / printed p. 15).

**Article 6, PDF p. 4:** the founding contribution is **EUR 500 in cash**, not vineyards. The annex of transactions undertaken for the company in formation, **PDF p. 17**, says **Neant** (none). The formation file therefore supplies no vineyard contribution schedule or executed vineyard lease. A company object permitting vineyard ownership and operation is not evidence that it farmed a specific parcel.
