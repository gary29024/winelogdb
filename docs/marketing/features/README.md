# WineLog feature posts

These are 11 single-image Instagram posts for **@winelog.app**, one per feature.
Each is 2160×2700 (4:5 portrait), saved as a standard JPEG. The phone screens
are real WineLog screens with sample data; the wines, producers and people are
made up.

Post one or two a week after the launch posts. Before each post, check the
caption against what the app does at that time.

Hashtags to add as the first comment on every post:
#WineLog #WineLover #WineJournal #WineTasting #WineApp #WineNotes #Vino

---

### 01 · Group Scan (`01-group-scan.jpg`)
> A whole table of bottles? One photo is enough. 📸🍷
>
> Photograph the line-up and WineLog finds each different wine, crops each bottle, and keeps every wine separate so you can check it before saving.
>
> WineLog is in open beta. Link in bio.

### 02 · Batch Scan (`02-batch-scan.jpg`)
> Back from the wine shop with a full bag? 🛍️
>
> Batch Scan lets you add photos for each bottle, queue them all, and close the app. WineLog reads the labels in the background, and you confirm each wine when it's ready.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 03 · Smart Search (`03-smart-search.jpg`)
> "What was that silky red we had at dinner?" 🤔
>
> Type a description instead of a name and Smart Search finds the closest matches in your journal. You can still search by name, region or grape too.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 04 · Share with friends (`04-share-with-friends.jpg`)
> Great wine is better shared. 🥂
>
> Tag friends on a bottle and they can see the wine, its details and photos. You choose who sees what, and everyone keeps their own notes and rating.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 05 · Tasting evenings (`05-tasting-evenings.jpg`)
> Remember the whole night, not just one bottle. 🍽️
>
> Start a tasting and every wine you log joins it, with the date and venue already filled in, in the order you poured them. Tag friends to share the whole evening.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 06 · Wine lists (`06-wine-lists.jpg`)
> Snap the list. Keep the prices. 🧾
>
> At a tasting or restaurant, photograph the wine list or handout. WineLog reads the wines and prices, matches them to your evening, and lets you add anything missing.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 07 · Your cellar (`07-cellar.jpg`)
> How many bottles of that 2019 do you have left? 🍾
>
> Keep track of what you own: how many bottles, what you paid and where each one is stored. When you open one, WineLog logs the tasting and takes it off the count.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 08 · Drinking window (`08-drinking-window.jpg`)
> Too young, ready, or time to drink up? ⏳
>
> Every bottle in your cellar shows whether it's ready, with the years to drink it. For more detail, look up the vintage to get a WineLog score and sources.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 09 · Producers (`09-producers.jpg`)
> Meet the people behind the bottle. 👩‍🌾
>
> Every producer gets a profile with their story and how they farm, plus every wine of theirs you've tasted, grouped by cuvée and vintage.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 10 · Vintages (`10-vintages.jpg`)
> Why was 2019 so good in Gevrey-Chambertin? ☀️
>
> See how each season shaped the wine, village by village: heat, rain, harvest date, and how the year compares with 1991–2020.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.

### 11 · Collections (`11-collections.jpg`)
> How many have you tasted? 🏅
>
> Work through wine checklists, from Burgundy's 33 Grand Crus to the ten Beaujolais Crus. They fill in automatically as you log, or you can build your own around a favourite producer or region.
>
> Open beta. Link in bio.
