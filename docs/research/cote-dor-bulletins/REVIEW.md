# Côte-d'Or notice review, 2016–2020 publications

Reviewed 29 September 2026. The [review catalog](review-catalog.json) connects this
curated evidence to the completed departmental index and pins its checksums.

## Results

[reviewed-parcels.json](reviewed-parcels.json) contains **186 decision/parcel rows
from 14 image-reviewed decisions**, representing **184 distinct printed
commune/reference pairs**. Twelve decisions concern vineyards, land for vines or a
vineyard-named applicant; two additional decisions preserve competing Merceuil
applications whose transferred land is not established as vineyard. Two rows retain
an unresolved commune code. This is historical administrative evidence, not a list
of 184 verified present-day farmers.

The entire **31,966-page** text corpus was searched without a commune restriction.
[review-audit.json](review-audit.json) records all **130** modern title matches:
**57** are agricultural farm-structure notices, including **9** whose parcel evidence
is now image-reviewed; **73** concern other permits. The other **48** agricultural
notices were screened in text but are not labelled image-reviewed. Five legacy
decisions were recovered from the full page text, outside those modern matches.
A supplementary vineyard/parcel keyword search returned **87 pages**; the audit
preserves the expressions, input hash and every hit.

The search can miss wording split across pages or damaged by OCR. The five unresolved
2018 links remain a coverage gap. No claim of exhaustive farmer identification is made.

## Findings that affect identification

- **Competing approvals:** Dury Millot and Meix Guillot each received approval for
  Merceuil ZN 15 and ZN 232. Benjamin Pidet is also discussed as a late competing
  applicant not subject to the control. These documents do not establish which
  applicant obtained a lease or actually farmed. Both approvals remain separate rows.
- **Distinct roles:** Christiane Bachey is the person providing parcels under a
  long-term lease. She is recorded as lessor, not silently promoted to previous farmer.
  Unnamed incumbent tenants remain null. SA Boisset is a non-operating associate in
  the Bouillot notice, not an inferred previous operator.
- **Scope:** Bouillot's 16.1082 ha covers five communes; Vougeraie's 0.5092 ha covers
  three. Neither total belongs exclusively to the first commune in the sentence.
  Mouchottes' parcel land use is unspecified in the operative wording despite its name.
- **Names and historical references:** the Vignerons de Coeur title says `LES`, while
  its body and Article 1 say `DES`; the body name is retained. `MOLESMES` is retained
  as printed with two null commune codes, pending confirmation of a possible Molesme
  spelling error. Cheilly's `O 198` is retained as printed; no current cadastral match
  is asserted. An unrelated Hautes Côtes notice has `ZA 3,23` in its recital and
  `ZE 3,23` in Article 1; the audit flags that conflict without manufacturing a match.
- **Receipt is not an established outcome:** four 2019 receipts describe possible
  implicit approval after four months, with possible extension to six. The audit
  retains `application-received`; elapsed time alone does not prove authorisation.

## Additional leads

[reviewed-context.json](reviewed-context.json) keeps two reviewed notices outside the
farmer attribution dataset:

- **Pernand-Vergelesses, Sur Forêts (2 September 2019):** environmental derogation
  for a planting project capped at 7.7 ha. Article 3 lists 21 AI references, including
  AI 471 only in part. Twelve other AI references concern ecological compensation,
  not planting. The municipality is the permit beneficiary; no farming domaine is
  named. The municipal lease allocation and final planting plan are useful next sources.
- **Saint-Romain–Le Jarron (19 November 2019):** transformation of an owners
  association, with planned parcel consolidation and vineyard works. Gilles Buisson
  is named as an owner and temporary administrator. The parcel schedule, plan and
  later consolidation/lease records could provide a historical crosswalk; that role
  does not identify a farmer for every parcel.

## Reuse and validation

Use [review-catalog.json](review-catalog.json) as the entry point for this review.
The raw `index/notices.json` retains its machine-generated `unreviewed` status.
Join curated modern sources and audit entries by `noticeId`; also append the five
legacy sources ending in `:reviewed`, which have no modern act ID. Do not overwrite
the regional index or flatten repeated decisions into one producer assignment.

The departmental schema is now version 2: `sources` holds notice-level facts and
printed reference groups; top-level `parcels` follows PR #410's flat row approach.
This replaces the initial four nested Dubreuil rows, preserving their facts and using
the shared `authorised` status. Commune codes are supported by the checked-in DGFiP
name dictionary and, for department 71, the cited official DRAAF commune list.
Normalization expands a shared printed section and pads the parcel number; it does
not establish cadastral continuity, present geometry, producer identity or current farming.

```powershell
python scripts/validate_cotedor_reviews.py
python -m unittest discover -s scripts -p '*bulletin*.py'
```

The validator checks source hashes and pages against the committed full text,
commune assignments, reference expansion, dates, per-notice counts, row consistency,
review dispositions and checksums. It rejects allocating a notice total to multiple
parcels or converting a historical notice into a verified current farmer. After an
intentional review edit, use `--write-catalog` to validate and refresh review checksums.
