# Clos de Vougeot: public company filings

Reviewed 7 October 2026 for [Tier 2 issue #424](https://github.com/gary29024/winelogdb/issues/424). Filings came from the free index at entreprises.lefigaro.fr, with PDFs served by actes.ccm2.net. Pages were screened by embedded text or, for scans, OCR on page images; cited schedules were checked against the page text and, for the key contribution schedules, the page image. Original PDFs and scratch text are not committed.

Dates separate the deed or decision (`documentDate`) from the deposit (`filingDate`). Page numbers count PDF pages, including the registry cover. SHA-256 hashes cover the downloaded raw bytes; retrieval times are in the [curation](curation.json) or the [shared holder table](../holders/holder-links.json).
