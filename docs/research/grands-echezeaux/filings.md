Reviewed 1 October 2026 for [Tier 2 issue #423](https://github.com/gary29024/winelogdb/issues/423). Original PDFs and scratch OCR/images are not committed.

Dates below separate the transaction/report/meeting from the deposit. Page numbers count PDF pages, including covers. SHA-256 hashes cover the downloaded raw bytes; the machine-readable [curation](curation.json) also stores exact UTC retrieval times and byte counts. Local review dates use Asia/Taipei, so a 30 September UTC capture can be reviewed on 1 October locally.

## Priority holder triage

- **SEPV / Drouhin:** the 1994 vineyard schedule and completed capital resolutions move the lead beyond shared management. The 2022 statutes explicitly name a majority interest in Maison Joseph Drouhin (515620417). No current D103 lease or actual-operation confirmation was found.
- **Lamarche SCEA:** its contribution proves recorded ownership context. Public Winehog previews report the 2022 Liger-Belair arrangement; the free [Decanter 2022 score table](https://www.decanter.com/burgundy-2022-red-wine-score-table/) explicitly calls it métayage. The [April 2026 suspension, pp. 28–30](https://www.prefectures-regions.gouv.fr/bourgogne-franche-comte/irecontenu/telechargement/137449/1004181/file/recueil-bfc-2026-067-recueil-des-actes-administratifs-nominatifs.pdf#page=28) is a separate administrative fact, not evidence of current operation or an executed replacement lease.
- **SCI Dom Veuve Paul Modot:** Article 3 gives the exact abbreviation and office for SIREN 778173500; older statutes agree. The Schenk shareholders and Villamont’s own Groupe Schenk legal notice support estate context. The addresses use different street numbers (2 versus 5). No specific lease was found; the provisional DGFiP holder ID U18178008 and historical identifier uncertainty remain intact.
- **Romanée-Conti:** the [rendered estate page](https://www.romanee-conti.fr/fr/9-grand-crus/4/grands-echezeaux) was read and states 3.5263 ha. The 2014 free article’s annotated aerial has no cadastral numbers. Corporate wine/brand inventories are not land schedules.
