# Musigny Tier 2: source and filing inventory

Reviewed 8 October 2026 for season 2026. All 16 holders were checked, largest Musigny parcel groups first. Nine have reviewed company links; seven have documented no-link searches. A link is a research lead, not verified farming.

## Scope and method

24 newly selected free filings / 586 pages. Every page received RapidOCR with DirectML at 150 dpi; embedded text was also inspected. The SEPV and Perrot-Minot exact schedules, Tawse share allocation and Faiveley presidency were checked against page images. Raw PDFs, OCR, article bytes and metadata remain outside the repository. Deed dates are distinct from deposit dates. Selection is bounded, not an exhaustive company archive. Prior shared links/searches/effort are reused separately.
