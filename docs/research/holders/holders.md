Holder research is done once per legal holder, not once per cru. Each link connects a recorded legal holder (SIREN or provisional DGFiP identifier) to a domaine or producer through a stated company relation and its sources. It is shown as **Research link · farming unverified**: a relation between companies never establishes who farms a parcel. Legal holder, applicant, operator and bottler stay distinct.
