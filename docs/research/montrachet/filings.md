# Montrachet: public company filings

Reviewed 9 October 2026 for [Tier 2 #446](https://github.com/gary29024/winelogdb/issues/446), following [Richebourg #530](https://github.com/gary29024/winelogdb/pull/530). Every one of the 15 recorded holder identifiers was searched, most parcels first. Shared Bouchard, Pousse d’Or and DRC links and prior effort were reused without rewriting them.

**50 distinct filings / 1,360 pages** were screened in this bounded selection of formation, contribution, donation, transformation and recent statutes. PyMuPDF extraction was followed by every-page rendering at 150 dpi and RapidOCR with ONNX Runtime DirectML. Positive schedules and lease recitals were checked against page images. A negative means no qualifying match in these files, not that none exists.

52 successful PDF requests contained two byte-identical duplicates (34 pages); these are not counted twice. One further Leflaive statutes request returned HTTP 408. INPI and Pappers each returned HTTP 403 on one local attempt. No PDFs, OCR, article copies or archives are committed. The original URL, retrieval time, SHA-256, byte count, page count and distinct document/deposit dates are recorded below and in the shared holder table.
